## Applicability Boundaries

GDPR duties depend on territorial/material scope, the processing operation, and controller/processor role. EU/EEA guidance is not automatically UK GDPR guidance. California CCPA duties apply to covered businesses and relevant personal information, subject to statutory thresholds and exemptions; they do not apply to every website. HIPAA Security Rule duties apply to covered entities and business associates handling ePHI, not to every health-data application. HHS explicitly identifies its summary as the currently effective rule and links proposed modifications separately; proposals were not treated as enacted obligations.

SOC2 denotes an assurance engagement/report, not an automatic certification or a general statute. Its scoped criteria and commitments require engagement-specific evaluation. PCI DSS is a payment-data security standard; applicability, validation, and consequences depend on the relevant payment program and contracts, with any separate legal obligations assessed independently. Implementing a listed control does not establish compliance with any complete framework.

- **G3:** [EDPB international transfers guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/international-data-transfers_en). Reviewed transfer criteria, adequacy, Article 46 safeguards, SCC transfer assessments/supplementary measures, and exceptional Article 49 derogations.
- **G4:** [EDPB breach guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/assess-the-risks/data-breaches_en). Reviewed controller/processor differences, awareness, risk thresholds, 72-hour authority notice, phased notification, individual communication/exceptions, and documenting all breaches.
- **G5:** [EDPB compliance guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/be-compliant_en). Reviewed protection by design/default and DPIA screening, contents, prior consultation, and reassessment. Records of processing were discussed but not added as a fourth topic.

- **C1:** [California Civil Code 1798.130](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1798.130.&lawCode=CIV). Subsection (a)(2)(A) supports the 45-day deletion response and one further 45-day reasonably necessary extension with notice during the initial period. Verification does not extend the initial clock.
- **C2:** [California Attorney General CCPA overview](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa). Supports conditional deletion rights, sale/sharing opt-out, GPC, and collection notices. Its FAQ disclaims legal advice and contains apparently stale statements about some regulations; those statements were not adopted as current law.
